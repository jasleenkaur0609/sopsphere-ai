# 🤖 AI SOP Portal

### AI-Powered Enterprise Knowledge & Standard Operating Procedure Management Platform

> 🚧 **Project Status: In Development**

The **AI SOP Portal** is an enterprise-focused web application designed to centralize Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), knowledge management, user management, approvals, training, compliance, reporting, and AI-powered document intelligence into a single platform.

The platform is being developed with a modern, scalable architecture where users can access features based on their assigned organizational role.

# 📖 Project Overview

The AI SOP Portal is being developed as a centralized enterprise platform for managing organizational knowledge and Standard Operating Procedures.

Instead of maintaining SOPs, documents, approvals, training information, and compliance activities across multiple disconnected systems, the portal aims to provide a single platform for managing the complete SOP lifecycle.

The platform will combine:

- 📄 SOP Management
- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 👥 User & Role Management
- 🔐 Authentication & Authorization
- 📚 Knowledge Management
- ✅ Approval Workflows
- 🎓 Training & Compliance
- 📊 Reports & Analytics
- 🔎 Intelligent Document Search
- 📑 Document Processing
- 🔔 Notifications
- ⚙️ Administration

The system is being designed with scalability and role-based access control in mind.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Organizations often manage SOPs and operational knowledge using multiple tools such as:

- SharePoint
- Excel
- Email
- Word/PDF documents
- Local folders
- Manual approval processes
- Separate reporting systems

This can result in:

- Difficulty finding the latest SOP
- Duplicate documents
- Manual approval tracking
- Poor visibility into SOP status
- Difficult compliance monitoring
- Lack of centralized ownership
- Manual reporting
- Limited intelligent document analysis

The AI SOP Portal aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized and intelligent platform.

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of the platform are:

### 1. Centralized SOP Management

Provide a single location to create, manage, review, approve, publish, and maintain SOPs.

### 2. Role-Based Access

Users should only see the functionality and information relevant to their organizational role.

### 3. AI-Powered Knowledge Management

Use AI to assist with:

- SOP generation
- SOP summarization
- Document comparison
- Knowledge search
- Document classification
- Compliance checking
- Information extraction

### 4. Workflow Automation

Reduce manual work through automated:

- Approvals
- Notifications
- Document processing
- Training workflows
- Compliance checks

### 5. Enterprise Visibility

Provide dashboards and reports for different levels of the organization.
